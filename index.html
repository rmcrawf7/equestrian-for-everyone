Jenn Crawford founded Equestrian for Everyone to create opportunities, break down barriers, and build a more accessible and inclusive equestrian community—where riders of all abilities can experience the partnership, independence, confidence and joy that horses provide.

Meet the Founder

Jenn Crawford is the heart behind Equestrian for Everyone.

Jenn’s own return to riding after a life-changing accident shaped a mission centred on adaptation, representation and creating a place in equestrian sport for riders of every ability. Her story leads the EFE story—and the growing community around it.

Our Mission

Equestrian sport should be for everyone.

Our mission is to foster a welcoming environment where people of all abilities, backgrounds, and ages can benefit from the therapeutic, educational, and recreational aspects of equestrian sports.

Why EFE Exists

A different way forward.

For Jenn Crawford, the mission is personal. After a life-changing riding accident, returning to horses meant adapting, rebuilding and finding a new path in the sport she loved.

That experience grew into Equestrian for Everyone: a community focused on opportunity, education, representation and making sure disability does not have to mean the end of an equestrian journey.
