Jenn Crawford

Every rider has a different path. This is Jenn’s story.

← Meet the Riders

Jenn was five years old when horses changed her life. Years later, she found her special partner in Beau, an abandoned, severely underweight gelding. After six months of rehabilitation, Jenn began riding him in 2018, quickly discovering his talent and dreaming of competing together.

That same year, a riding accident fractured Jenn’s L1 and T12 vertebra, leaving her unable to move her legs. Doctors warned that walking again was unlikely. Determined to ride Beau again, Jenn spent six weeks in spinal cord rehabilitation working toward one goal. Six months after her accident, she climbed back into the saddle.

Since then, Jenn and Beau have continued to grow as a team, competing successfully in dressage and jumping. She competes in the Silver Series at Angelstone in the 0.8m and 0.9m divisions and has represented Para Equestrian sport at BreyerFest as part of the Para Showjumping North America Team.

Jenn’s journey is a testament to perseverance, partnership, and the power of adaptive riding.
